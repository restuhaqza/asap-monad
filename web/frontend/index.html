How a fire gets answered

Four steps, one block.

Detect

An oracle commits the hash of the raw FIRMS file, then publishes the parsed rows. Anyone can re-download it and prove it was not altered.

HotspotRegistry
Fund

Whoever carries the risk opens a bounty on that coordinate: a concession, a carbon project, a CSR budget, a local disaster agency.

escrow in MON
Respond

A responder stakes a bond and submits geotagged evidence — a photo, hashed and stored content-addressed. The contract enforces radius and time.

bond ≥ pool
Settle

Two verifiers attest and the payout clears. Two rejections slash the bond and reopen the bounty. Nobody shows up, and the expiry is public.

A bond punishes someone after the land has burned. A bounty pays someone to reach the smoke while it is still small. Both belong in the same country; only one of them moves a person.

Why it does not get gamed

A fake report has to be more expensive than it is worth.

  • ✓Bond equals the pool. Claiming costs as much as the reward, so lying is strictly unprofitable — and the honest responder gets it back.
  • ✓Evidence cannot predate the satellite pass. The contract checks the capture timestamp against the hotspot's acquisition time.
  • ✓Distance is enforced on-chain. A flat-earth approximation within 8° of the equator, accurate to about 1% — 25 km radius.
  • ✓Expiry is a feature. A bounty nobody answered stays on the public record as a fire that was funded and ignored.

What this does not do yet

Satellite data lags.

NRT is hours behind reality, and we never claim a fire is burning right now — only that it was detected in the last 24 hours.

Trusted quorum.

Verification is two whitelisted addresses today. v2 is stake-weighted verifiers with an appeal window.

Bond excludes the poorest.

A 100% bond makes fraud unprofitable, and also means a volunteer with no capital cannot respond. A cooperative bond pool is the fix.
